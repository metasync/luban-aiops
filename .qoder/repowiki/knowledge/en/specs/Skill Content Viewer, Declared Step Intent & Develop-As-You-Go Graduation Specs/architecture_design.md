Each subdirectory is a self-contained spec package (`spec.md` + `plan.md` + `tasks.md`) following the repository's ADR-0008 delivery format with Status/Summary/Motivation/Requirements/Acceptance-Criteria/Non-Goals/Impact/Changelog sections. The three specs form a sequential program: SPEC-052 adds a read-only gateway detail proxy and portal `SkillContentViewer` modal over existing skills-hub endpoints; SPEC-053 extends that path with an additive `flow_intent` frontmatter key carried through tool-gateway `FlowState`, agent-platform `FlowContext.summary()`, and the portal confirmation card; SPEC-055 builds on SPEC-054 to introduce a durable authoring-trace store, an executable-flow skill class decoupling `risk_class` from `web_target`, deterministic blast-radius re-validation, and one-gate replay — plus R-7 hardening of the approval-seam secret-masking surface. Dependency direction is strictly forward: 052 → 053 → 055, with 055 explicitly gated on SPEC-054's `delivered` status and referencing ADR-0009 as its design anchor. All three are marked `status: delivered` in their Status blocks and were released under R5 slices (v0.34.0 for 052/053, v0.36.0 for 055).
