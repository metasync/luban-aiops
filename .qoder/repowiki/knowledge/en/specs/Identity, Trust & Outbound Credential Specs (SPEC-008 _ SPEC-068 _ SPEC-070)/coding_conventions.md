- Specs follow a fixed scaffold of `spec.md` + `plan.md` + `tasks.md` per directory, with status, owner, release slice, related ADRs, Summary, Motivation, Requirements (numbered R-N with Acceptance criteria), Non-Goals, Impact, Open Questions, and Changelog sections.
- Requirements are written as numbered R-N blocks each followed by an indented Acceptance criteria list, making every requirement independently testable once the spec reaches `approved`.
- Cross-spec changes are expressed as additive extensions to prior specs (e.g. SPEC-070 explicitly extends SPEC-068's `credential_set` mechanism; SPEC-068 extends SPEC-049 R-5) rather than replacements, preserving backward compatibility of existing `basic` sets.
- Secret handling is enforced through the shared `make validate-secret-vocabulary` check against canonical redaction vocabularies (`url_redaction.SECRET_QUERY_PARAMS`, `redaction._VALUE_PATTERNS`) rather than ad-hoc field lists, keeping secret-name additions free of validator churn.
- Delivery gates are split into development acceptance (unit/integration tests, `make verify` green) and production/live-system evidence (separate DI tasks such as DI-070), with operator-approved amendments recorded in the Changelog instead of editing ratified requirements.
