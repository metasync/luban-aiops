Three independent spec directories under `docs/specs/`, each following the project's standard plan/spec/tasks triad and tracking lifecycle metadata (status, owner, created, release slice, related specs). SPEC-019 defines a read-only transparency layer on top of existing backend services: a new `GET /api/v1/policy/matrix` endpoint in platform-gateway backed by the policy engine, plus proxied reads to tool-gateway (`/api/v2/tools`) and skills-hub (`/skills`) surfaced as Permissions, Tools, and Skills views behind a sectioned sidebar. SPEC-023 specifies a full presentation-layer rebuild of `products/operator-portal/web-ui` from vanilla JS to React + Ant Design X + TypeScript built via Vite, introducing a single platform-owned SSE contract adapter that translates stream v6 frames into typed models consumed by all views; it preserves all existing backend contracts, policy actions, and audit behavior while adding multi-session workspace UI and voice input. SPEC-033 is a targeted fix to the HITL confirmation pipeline: an additive `turn_index` column persisted at park time, exposed through session-detail/inbox record models, and used by the portal transcript seeding to anchor cards to their parking turn with a legacy newest-turn fallback. All three specs are marked `delivered` and reference shared contracts under `shared/shared-contracts/schemas/` and `policies/policy-default.yaml` as the authoritative boundary between portal and backend.
