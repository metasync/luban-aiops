`make deploy-samples` installs all samples or `make deploy-samples SAMPLE=web-checks/password-reset` installs a single one; `make undeploy-samples` removes the ConfigMap and restarts skills-hub. The password-reset demo runs deterministically after `make deploy` + `make deploy-samples`, with an opt-in chat leg via `RUN_CHAT=true bash samples/web-checks/password-reset/demo/demo.sh`. Prerequisites include a running dev-k8s cluster, the `browser-dev` runtime profile, and port-forwards to `identity-service` (18081) and `platform-gateway` (18083).
