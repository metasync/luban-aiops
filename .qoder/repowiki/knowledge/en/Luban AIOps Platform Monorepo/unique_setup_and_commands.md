`make verify` runs the full pre-commit/pre-push gate (per-product tests, Kustomize overlay rendering, policy-schema validation, and version lockstep check); `make build` tags every product image with a coordinated semver+sha tag and writes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `make deploy` applies the dev-k8s overlay; `make e2e` executes skills, incident, and mutating demos against the running cluster.
