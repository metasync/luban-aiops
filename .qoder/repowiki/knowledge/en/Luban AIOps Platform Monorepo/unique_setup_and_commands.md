Root `make verify` runs the full pre-commit gate (per-product tests, kustomize overlay renders, policy validation/scenarios, version lockstep). `make build` builds all images with one coordinated tag and writes `.images.env`; `make deploy` applies the dev-k8s overlay; `make e2e` runs the demo scripts in `shared/platform-ops/e2e/`; `make sync-policy` copies the canonical policy bundle to both gateway products and the dev overlay.
