The repo is a flat product monorepo under `products/` where each service owns its own src/tests/build artifacts and exposes an HTTP API; cross-service communication goes through the `platform-gateway`, which enforces policy and forwards to downstream services (agent-platform, identity-broker, audit-service, incident-service, skills-hub, tool-gateway). Inter-service boundaries are frozen as JSON schemas in `shared/shared-contracts/schemas/` and validated at build/test time. Feature scope and evolution are governed by numbered specs in `docs/specs/SPEC-NNN-*` (plan/spec/tasks triad) and ADRs in `docs/adr/`. Deployment is GitOps-driven via `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/kustomization.yaml` overlays plus per-product Dockerfiles, with runtime profiles under `runtime-profiles/` selecting provider secrets. The top-level `Makefile` and `mk/*.mk` provide common image/Python build targets consumed by every product's Makefile.
