The service is assembled in `app.py` by creating a FastAPI instance, attaching a request-logging middleware (via `core.request_context.resolve_request_id`), including the central `api.router.router`, then wiring Prometheus metrics and OpenTelemetry telemetry. Each route module under `api/routes/` (auth, chat, documents, sessions, skills, tools, incidents, approvals, audit, identity, policy, runtime) performs three steps: resolve identity via `services.token_verifier` / `services.delegation_client`, evaluate permissions through `services.policy_engine` against the YAML bundle in `policies/policy-default.yaml`, then forward the call to one of the downstream clients (`agent_client`, `incident_client`, `skills_hub_client`, `tool_gateway_client`). Cross-cutting concerns are centralized in `core/` (config, metrics, observability, request context, runtime settings) and emitted audit events go through `services.audit_emitter`. The entry point `main.py` boots uvicorn with `GatewayRunSettings.from_env()`, making environment-driven configuration the single source of truth for host/port and all downstream service endpoints.
