All cross-cutting operations are invoked from the root `Makefile`: `make verify` runs every product's tests plus Kustomize overlay rendering, policy validation, scenario checks, version lockstep, and secret-vocabulary validation; `make build` produces coordinated images tagged `<semver>-<gitsha>` and writes `.images.env`; `make deploy` runs `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh`; `make e2e` executes demo scripts against a deployed cluster after port-forwarding `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081`; `make sync-policy` copies the canonical policy bundle into both gateways and the dev overlay.
