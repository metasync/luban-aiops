`make verify` is the authoritative pre-commit/pre-push gate running every product test suite, kustomize overlay rendering, dashboard validation, policy bundle validation/scenario checks, version/secret-vocabulary/password-policy lockstep checks, secret-delivery demo, portal unit tests + production build, and the crash-safety execution failure test. `make build` produces coordinated images tagged `<semver>-<prefix>[-<profile>]-<sha>` and writes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `make deploy` applies the dev-k8s overlay; `make e2e` runs the full e2e demo suite against a deployed cluster requiring port-forwards to platform-gateway and identity-service.
