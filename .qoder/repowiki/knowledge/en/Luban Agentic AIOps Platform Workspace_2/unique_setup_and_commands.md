`make verify` is the canonical gate — runs every product test suite, kustomize overlay render check, dashboard validation, policy/schema/version/password-policy validations, secret-delivery demo, portal Vitest + tsc build, and delegated-custody/runtime/multiproc/edge integration proofs. `make build` produces a coordinated IMAGE_TAG across all products and writes `.images.env`; `make deploy` applies `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s`; `make e2e` runs the full demo suite against a running cluster with port-forwards.
