`make verify` is the canonical pre-commit/pre-push gate running product tests, Kustomize overlay render, dashboard validation, policy scenario evaluation, VERSION/secret-vocabulary/password-policy checks, portal unit+build, audit-contract test, and delegated-custody/runtime/multiproc/edge + execution-failure integration proofs. `make build` produces coordinated images tagged from the root `VERSION` file and writes `.images.env` consumed by deploy; `make e2e` runs the scripted demos against a deployed cluster after port-forwarding platform-gateway and identity-service.
