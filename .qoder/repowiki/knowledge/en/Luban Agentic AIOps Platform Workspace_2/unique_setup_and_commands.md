The root `Makefile` is the single entry point: `make verify` runs all product test suites, renders every Kustomize overlay, validates the canonical policy bundle against JSON schema, evaluates policy scenarios, and enforces version lockstep between `VERSION`, product versions, and the portal; `make build` produces coordinated image tags written to `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `make deploy` invokes the dev-k8s deploy script; `make e2e` runs end-to-end demo scripts against a deployed cluster after port-forwarding `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081`; `make sync-policy` copies the canonical policy bundle into both gateway packages and the dev-k8s ConfigMap source.
