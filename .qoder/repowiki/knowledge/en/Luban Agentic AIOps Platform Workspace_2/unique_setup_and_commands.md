`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate running every product test suite, Kustomize overlay rendering, policy/scenario validation, version lockstep, secret-vocabulary checks, and the local secret-delivery demo. `make build` produces coordinated images tagged `<semver>-<prefix>[-<profile>]-<gitsha>` (dirty builds append `-dirty-<timestamp>`) and writes them to `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`. `make deploy` applies the dev-k8s overlay; `make e2e` runs the full demo suite against a live cluster after port-forwarding `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081`. Per-product commands are invoked via `make -C products/<name> <target>`.
