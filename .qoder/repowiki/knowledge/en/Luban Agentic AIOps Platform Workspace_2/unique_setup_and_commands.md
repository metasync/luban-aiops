`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate and runs every product test suite plus overlay rendering, dashboard validation, policy scenario checks, version/secret-vocabulary/password-policy validation, secret-delivery demo, and portal Vitest/tsc build. `make build` produces coordinated images tagged `<semver>-[prefix][-profile]-<sha>` and writes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `make deploy` applies the dev-k8s overlay. `make e2e` runs the full demo suite against a deployed cluster with port-forwards to platform-gateway:18083 and identity-service:18081. Policy artifacts are synchronized via `make sync-policy` from `shared/shared-contracts/policies/policy-default.yaml`.
