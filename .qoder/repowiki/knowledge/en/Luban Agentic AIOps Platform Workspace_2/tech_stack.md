Python services managed with `uv` (single interpreter pinned by `.python-version` at repo and per-product level), containerized on a shared `luban-aiops/base-uv` Amazon Linux 2023 image; Kubernetes deployment via Kustomize overlays under `shared/platform-ops/gitops`; operator portal is a Node SPA served by nginx; observability dashboards are config-as-code OpenObserve manifests validated offline.
