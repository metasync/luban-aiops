The repository is a multi-product monorepo organized around stable architectural boundaries rather than technology layers. `products/` holds independently buildable, containerized services (agent-platform, audit-service, execution-runtime, identity-broker, incident-service, platform-gateway, skills-hub, tool-gateway, operator-portal) each with its own `pyproject.toml`, `.python-version`, `Dockerfile`, `Makefile`, `src/<package>/`, and `tests/`. `shared/` contains cross-cutting assets: `shared-contracts/` (API/event/policy schemas, SQL, scripts), `shared-sdk/` (client libraries), and `shared/platform-ops/` (Kubernetes GitOps overlays under `gitops/`, OpenObserve dashboards, e2e demo scripts). `docs/` is split into `adr/` (architecture decision records), `specs/` (per-feature plan/spec/task triplets driving development), `agentic-aiops-platform/` (design docs + release notes), `guides/`, and `workspace/` (spikes and internal studies). The root `Makefile` is the single orchestration entry point: it declares `PYTHON_PRODUCTS` and `IMAGE_PRODUCTS`, delegates per-product `sync/test/lint/build/push` to each product's Makefile, coordinates image tagging from `VERSION`, renders kustomize overlays, validates policies/dashboards/version lockstep, runs secret-delivery and failure-safety proofs, and exposes `make verify` as the pre-commit/pre-push gate. Dependency direction is inward toward `shared/`; products communicate via the contracts in `shared/shared-contracts` rather than direct coupling.
