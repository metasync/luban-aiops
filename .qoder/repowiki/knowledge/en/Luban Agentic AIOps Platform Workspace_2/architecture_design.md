The root is a forge-agnostic orchestrator: `Makefile` declares the product surface (`PYTHON_PRODUCTS`, `IMAGE_PRODUCTS`) and delegates per-product build/test/lint to each `products/<name>/Makefile`, while owning cross-cutting concerns — coordinated image tagging from the single `VERSION` file, policy bundle sync from `shared/shared-contracts/policies/`, GitOps overlay validation via kustomize, dashboard schema validation, and the `verify` pre-commit/pre-push gate. Products communicate through the contract layer in `shared/shared-contracts/` (schemas, policies, SQL) rather than direct imports; `mk/{defaults.mk,image.mk,python.mk}` provides shared Docker and uv build fragments consumed by every product. The `docs/specs/SPEC-*` directory is the authoritative design ledger driving implementation, and `samples/acme-admin` exercises the deployed platform end-to-end without being part of the base overlay.
