The workspace is organized around product-oriented microservices under `products/`, each a self-contained FastAPI application with its own `src/<service>/api|core|schemas|services|tools`, `tests/`, `Dockerfile`, `pyproject.toml`, and `uv.lock`. Cross-cutting concerns live in `shared/shared-contracts` (JSON schemas for chat/session/stream/audit/tool-invocation/policy), `shared/shared-sdk`, and `shared/platform-ops/gitops` (Kustomize overlays). The root `Makefile` orchestrates the build pipeline: it iterates over `PYTHON_PRODUCTS` to run per-product `make test/sync/lint`, builds coordinated container images tagged from the single `VERSION` file, renders Kustomize overlays via `kustomize build`, validates the canonical policy bundle against scenario expectations, and deploys the `dev-k8s` overlay. Services communicate over HTTP/gRPC boundaries defined by the shared JSON schemas; the `platform-gateway` is the portal-facing edge (token verification, policy enforcement, session/chat proxying), `tool-gateway` normalizes tool/connector access (MCP, external systems) behind the same policy engine, `agent-platform` owns orchestration, sessions, streaming, and model providers, `identity-broker` issues JWTs verified via JWKS, `audit-service` persists durable events, `incident-service` ingests alerts and runs triage, `skills-hub` indexes Markdown skills, and `execution-runtime` runs bounded isolated actions. Policy decisions flow through a single canonical `policy-default.yaml` copied into both gateways and the dev-K8s ConfigMap via `make sync-policy`, with rollout controlled by `SPEC-048` scenarios validated at verify time.
