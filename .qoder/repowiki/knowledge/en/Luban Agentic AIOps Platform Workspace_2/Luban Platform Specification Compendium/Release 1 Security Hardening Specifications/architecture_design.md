Three sibling SPEC directories (`SPEC-001-release-1-platform-hardening`, `SPEC-003-identity-trust-hardening`, `SPEC-009-pre-production-hardening`) each follow a uniform three-file layout — `plan.md` (implementation plan), `spec.md` (requirements + acceptance criteria + impact), `tasks.md` (task breakdown). The specs form a staged dependency chain: SPEC-001 establishes gateway auth enforcement, role propagation, session integrity, contract validation, resilience, and CI; SPEC-003 replaces the caller-asserted identity with RSA-signed JWTs issued by `identity-broker` and verified locally in `tool-gateway` via JWKS; SPEC-009 closes Release 1 closure gates by adding deterministic tool-output redaction at the gateway's `invoke_tool` choke point and replacing the static client secret with Kubernetes projected workload-identity-bound tokens at the broker exchange. All three specs target the same product surface (`products/tool-gateway`, `products/identity-broker`, `products/agent-platform`) and reference shared contracts under `shared/shared-contracts`; they are living-state artifacts whose status progresses through draft → approved → in-progress → delivered, with changelog entries recording delivery milestones.
