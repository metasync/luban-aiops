OAuth 2.0 flows used across specs: broker-mediated JWT delegation with `aud`/`act` claims (SPEC-008), OAuth2 `client_credentials` grant for machine grants (SPEC-068), and per-user OAuth2 authorization-code with PKCE S256 and server-side refresh-token renewal (SPEC-070). Durable custody uses gateway-owned Postgres with application-level authenticated encryption and separately mounted versioned keys.
