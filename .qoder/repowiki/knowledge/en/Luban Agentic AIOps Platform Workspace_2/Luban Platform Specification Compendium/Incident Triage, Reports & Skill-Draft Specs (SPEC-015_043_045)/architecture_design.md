Three sibling spec directories under `docs/specs/`, each following the project's plan/spec/tasks triple: a `spec.md` with Status/Summary/Motivation/Requirements/Acceptance-Criteria/Non-Goals/Impact/Changelog, plus companion `plan.md` and `tasks.md`. The specs form a chronological dependency chain rather than a code module:

- SPEC-015 (R3) introduces the canonical incident envelope (`incident.schema.json`) and triage report schema (`triage-report.schema.json`), the new `products/incident-service` FastAPI service with dual intake (Alertmanager webhook + manual operator POST), agent-driven triage via platform-gateway delegation, a pluggable `Connector` protocol with an audit sink, read-only tool-gateway connectors, and the Incidents portal view.
- SPEC-043 (R5 slice v0.25.0) extends the existing document substrate from SPEC-039 with a second `document_type=incident_report`, adding an incident-report assembler in agent-platform that reads incident-service through a bounded internal Basic-auth client and composes the validated triage report, connector dispatch outcomes, and linked session digest under the two-tier ownership posture.
- SPEC-045 (R5 slice v0.27.0) adds an incident-anchored skill-draft generator reusing the SPEC-044 session-scoped draft machinery, gated by a new `incident:skill_draft` policy action plus `incident:read`, producing a preview modal with Download/Discard but no durable draft record.

Dependency direction is strictly forward: SPEC-043 depends on SPEC-015's incident schemas and incident-service contract; SPEC-045 depends on both SPEC-015 (incident data) and SPEC-043 (the incident client bundle). No implementation code lives in this scope — it is purely authoritative design documentation.
