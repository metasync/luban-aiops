Each SPEC is a self-contained leaf under `docs/specs/SPEC-NNN-...` with the standard triad of `spec.md`, `plan.md`, and `tasks.md`. The three specs form a sequential R4 delivery chain:
- SPEC-032 adds a portal-only `usePendingDecisionPoll` hook that polls `GET /api/v1/sessions/{id}` while a pending confirmation card exists, reseeding turns via `reseedTurns` without new backend surfaces.
- SPEC-033 extends the durable record store and kernel park path to persist `turn_index` (0-based user-turn ordinal) so each confirmation anchors under its parking exchange rather than stacking under the newest turn; the additive field propagates through `agent-session.schema.json` into session-detail and inbox models.
- SPEC-034 is purely UI polish on top of the above: arrival highlight (`detectArrivalSpan` + `.turn-group.turn-arrived`), instant session-list refresh on applied decisions, Pending/History tabs in Approvals, separated inbox entries with structured provenance headers, and an expiry banner referencing `AGENT_HITL_CONFIRM_TIMEOUT`.
Dependency direction is one-way: 034 builds on 032's poll/reseed contract and 033's anchored cards; none introduce new backend contracts beyond the additive schema change in 033. All three are marked `delivered` and shipped as portal-only changes against `products/operator-portal/web-ui/app` (with 033 also touching `products/agent-platform`).
