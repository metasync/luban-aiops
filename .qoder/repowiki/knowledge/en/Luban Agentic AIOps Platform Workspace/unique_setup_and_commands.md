Each product under `products/` is independently bootstrapped via uv (`.python-version` pins the interpreter) and built/tested through its local `Makefile`; the workspace root `Makefile` delegates to product make targets. End-to-end demos are driven from `samples/acme-admin/demo-suite.sh` and `samples/deploy-samples.sh`, while operator deployments use the GitOps overlays in `shared/platform-ops/gitops/`.
