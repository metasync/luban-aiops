`make verify` is the authoritative pre-commit/pre-push gate combining product tests, Kustomize overlay rendering, dashboard validation, policy bundle checks, version lockstep, secret vocabulary, password policy, secret-delivery demo, portal Vitest suite, and crash-safety failure test. `make deploy` applies the `dev-k8s` overlay; `make e2e` runs the scripted demo suite after port-forwarding `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081`. Policy bundles are synchronized from `shared/shared-contracts/policies/` via `make sync-policy` before consumers can be validated.
