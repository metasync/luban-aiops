`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate and must pass locally and in CI; it runs all product tests, renders Kustomize overlays, validates policy bundles/scenarios, and enforces version lockstep. `make build` builds every product image with a coordinated tag derived from `VERSION` and writes `.images.env` consumed by `make deploy`, which invokes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh`. E2E demos require a deployed cluster plus port-forwards to `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081` before running `make e2e`. Samples are installed out-of-band via `make deploy-samples` so base overlays never reference them (SPEC-050 R-11). Policy synchronization uses `make sync-policy` to copy the canonical bundle from `shared/shared-contracts/policies/policy-default.yaml` into both gateway products and the dev overlay.
