`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate running every product test suite, Kustomize overlay render, dashboard validation, policy bundle checks, version lockstep, secret-vocabulary check, password-policy check, secret-delivery demo, portal Vitest/tsc build, audit contract test, delegated-custody/runtime/multiproc/edge integration tests, and execution-failure proof. `make build` produces coordinated images tagged `<semver>-<prefix>[-<profile>]-<sha>` (or `-dirty-<timestamp>` for dirty trees) and writes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `make deploy` applies the dev-k8s overlay; `make e2e` runs the scripted demos against a deployed cluster requiring port-forwards to `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081`.
