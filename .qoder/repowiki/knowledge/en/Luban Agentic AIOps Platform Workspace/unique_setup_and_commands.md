`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate and runs every product test suite, Kustomize overlay renders, policy scenario validations, version lockstep checks, secret-vocabulary validation, password-policy validation, the local secret-delivery demo, operator-portal Vitest + production build, and the SPEC-063 real-Postgres multiprocess failure-test. Use `make -C products/<name> help` for per-product targets; `make deploy` invokes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh`; `make e2e` runs the full demo suite against a deployed cluster requiring port-forwards to `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081`; `make secret-delivery-demo` runs the local handoff proof without a cluster.
