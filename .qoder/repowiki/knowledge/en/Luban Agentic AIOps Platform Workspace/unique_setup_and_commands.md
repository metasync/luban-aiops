`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate running every product's tests plus Kustomize overlay rendering, policy validation, and version lockstep checks. `make build` produces coordinated images tagged from `VERSION` and writes `.images.env` consumed by deploy; `make deploy` runs `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh`; `make e2e` executes demo scripts after port-forwarding `platform-gateway` and `identity-service`. Policy files must be synchronized across consumers via `make sync-policy` and validated with `make validate-policy`.
