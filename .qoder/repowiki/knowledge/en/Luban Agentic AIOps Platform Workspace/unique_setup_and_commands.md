`make verify` is the authoritative pre-commit/pre-push gate — it runs every product test suite, renders all Kustomize overlays, validates dashboards, policy bundles, version lockstep, secret vocabulary, password policy, the secret-delivery demo, the operator-portal SPA suite, audit contract tests, delegated custody/runtime/multiproc/edge integration tests, and execution failure tests. `make build` produces coordinated images with a single semver tag derived from `VERSION` and writes `.images.env` consumed by the GitOps deploy. `make e2e` runs the scripted demos against a deployed cluster after port-forwarding `platform-gateway` and `identity-service`.
