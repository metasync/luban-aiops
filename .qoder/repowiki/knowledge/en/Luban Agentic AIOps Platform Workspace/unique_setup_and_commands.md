Top-level workflow is driven by the root `Makefile`: `make verify` runs every product's test suite, renders all Kustomize overlays, validates the canonical policy bundle, and checks version lockstep; `make build` builds coordinated images tagged `<semver>-<prefix>[-<profile>]-<gitsha>` (dirty builds append timestamp) and writes them to `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `make deploy` invokes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh`; `make e2e` runs `skills-demo.sh`, `incident-demo.sh`, and `mutating-demo.sh` against a running dev cluster after port-forwarding `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081`; `make sync-policy` copies the canonical policy to both gateways and the base overlay; `make validate-version` enforces that `VERSION`, product versions, and portal versions stay in lockstep.
