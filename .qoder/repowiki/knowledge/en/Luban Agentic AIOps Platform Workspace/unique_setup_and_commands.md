`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate running every product test suite, Kustomize overlay render, policy scenario validation, version lockstep check, and secret-vocabulary validation; `make build` builds all product images with a coordinated semver tag derived from `VERSION` plus git state and writes `.images.env`; `make deploy` applies the `dev-k8s` overlay; `make e2e` runs the five demo scripts (skills, incident, mutating, http-check, acme-admin) against a deployed cluster after port-forwarding `platform-gateway` and `identity-service`.
