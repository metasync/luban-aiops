`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate running every product test suite, kustomize overlay render check, dashboard validation, policy bundle validation/scenario evaluation, version lockstep check, secret-vocabulary check, password-policy check, secret-delivery demo, portal unit+build, audit contract test, delegated-custody/runtime/multiproc/edge integration tests, and execution-failure test. `make build` produces all images with one coordinated tag and optionally loads them into a kind cluster; `make deploy` applies the `dev-k8s` overlay; `make e2e` runs the full demo suite against a deployed cluster.
