Two independent, versioned YAML bundles coexist under this package with no Python logic — they are data-only artifacts loaded at runtime by the gateway's policy engine.

- `policy-default.yaml` is the v1 action-authorization bundle (SPEC-048 / SPEC-030). It declares rules in an allow/deny/require_approval triad, scoped to the `action_authz` domain. Rules match on `roles_any` + `actions_any`, carry a numeric `priority`, and may include an `approval.tier` (`tier_1` self-approval vs. `tier_2` distinct approver) plus `decided_by_roles`. The bundle documents the precedence semantics enforced by the engine: deny-by-default, explicit deny overrides require_approval, higher priority wins within an outcome class, disabled rules ignored.
- `password-policy.yaml` is the v1 password-strength contract (SPEC-062 R-2), the single source of truth for `secrets.generate_password`. It exposes `min_length`, `hard_floor`, `required_classes`, `entropy_floor_bits`, and `exclude_ambiguous`; env overrides may only tighten it, never weaken below the declared floor.

Both files pin a `version` field that marks the *schema* revision (not rule-revision counters) and are validated by external validators (`validate_policy.py`, `validate-password-policy`). The package `__init__.py` merely identifies them as the SPEC-004 policy bundles; consumers load them via the gateway's `PasswordPolicyStore` / policy-engine loader rather than importing from here.
