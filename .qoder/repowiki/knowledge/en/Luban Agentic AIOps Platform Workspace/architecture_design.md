The repository is organized as a modular platform where each child in `products/` is an independently buildable, Dockerized service (agent-platform, platform-gateway, tool-gateway, identity-broker, skills-hub, audit-service, incident-service, execution-runtime, operator-portal) that communicates only through explicit API/event contracts defined in `shared/shared-contracts` (schemas, policies, SQL migrations). The root `Makefile` is the sole orchestrator: it declares `PYTHON_PRODUCTS` and `IMAGE_PRODUCTS`, builds all images with one coordinated `IMAGE_TAG` derived from `VERSION`, writes `.images.env` consumed by the GitOps overlay in `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s`, and exposes cross-cutting gates (`verify`, `sync-policy`, `validate-version`, `validate-secret-vocabulary`, `validate-password-policy`, `overlays`, `e2e`). Policy bundles are authored once in `shared/shared-contracts/policies` and synced to every consumer via `make sync-policy`; secret vocabulary and version strings are similarly enforced centrally. `docs/adr`, `docs/specs`, and `docs/agentic-aiops-platform/release-notes` form the spec-driven development record that drives task decomposition per feature slice.
