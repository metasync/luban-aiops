The repository is a multi-product monorepo organized around stable architectural boundaries. `products/` holds independently deployable services (agent-platform, identity-broker, platform-gateway, tool-gateway, audit-service, execution-runtime, incident-service, skills-hub, operator-portal), each with its own Python package, Dockerfile, Makefile, tests, and `.python-version`. `shared/` contains cross-cutting assets: `shared-contracts` (API/event/policy schemas and validation scripts), `shared-sdk`, and `platform-ops` (Kustomize GitOps overlays under `gitops/dev-k8s`). `docs/` captures the spec-driven process via numbered specs (`SPEC-NNN-*`), ADRs, release notes, and guides. The root `Makefile` is the single entry point that delegates per-product routines, builds coordinated container images tagged from the root `VERSION` file, renders Kustomize overlays, validates policy bundles against JSON schemas, and runs e2e demo scripts. Dependency direction is strictly outward: products depend on `shared/shared-contracts` and `shared/platform-ops`; nothing in `shared` depends back on products. Policy files are authored once in `shared/shared-contracts/policies` and synced to consumers via `make sync-policy`, enforcing a single source of truth.
