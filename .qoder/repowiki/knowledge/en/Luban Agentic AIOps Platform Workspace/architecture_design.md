The repository is a product-oriented monorepo organized around stable architectural boundaries: `products/` holds independently deployable services (agent-platform, identity-broker, platform-gateway, tool-gateway, audit-service, incident-service, execution-runtime, skills-hub, operator-portal), each with its own `src/<product>/`, `tests/`, `pyproject.toml`, `Dockerfile`, and `Makefile`; `shared/` contains cross-cutting assets — `shared-contracts` (API/event/policy schemas, SQL migrations, validation scripts), `shared-sdk` (client libraries), and `shared/platform-ops` (Kubernetes Kustomize overlays, e2e scripts, skill samples). The root `Makefile` is the single entry point that delegates per-product routines via `mk/defaults.mk`, `image.mk`, and `python.mk`, coordinating image builds under a unified `IMAGE_TAG` derived from `VERSION`, policy bundle sync from `shared/shared-contracts/policies/`, overlay rendering via `kustomize build`, and the `verify` gate that runs all product tests, policy validations, portal Vitest + tsc build, secret-delivery demo, and SPEC-063 failure-test. Dependency direction is strictly inward: products depend on `shared/shared-contracts` and `shared/shared-sdk` but never on each other; inter-service communication goes through explicit API/event contracts (e.g., audit events ingested by audit-service from gateway/tool-gateway/identity-broker). Spec-driven development is enforced by `docs/specs/SPEC-NNN/` directories (plan/spec/tasks) and `docs/adr/` decision records, with release notes in `docs/agentic-aiops-platform/release-notes/` tracking delivery slices.
