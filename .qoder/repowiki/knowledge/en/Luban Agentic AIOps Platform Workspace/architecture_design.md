The repository is organized as a modular multi-product workspace rather than a single application:
- `products/<name>/` holds independently versioned, containerized services (agent-platform, audit-service, execution-runtime, identity-broker, incident-service, platform-gateway, skills-hub, tool-gateway) plus the operator-portal SPA; each product ships its own `pyproject.toml`, `Dockerfile`, `Makefile`, `src/<package>/`, and `tests/`.
- `shared/shared-contracts/` is the canonical source of API/event/policy/schema definitions consumed by multiple products; `shared/platform-ops/` carries Kubernetes Kustomize overlays, dashboards, and e2e scripts; `shared/base-images/base-uv/` provides the shared Amazon Linux 2023 + uv base image.
- `docs/specs/SPEC-NNN-<title>/` follows a plan/spec/tasks triad per feature, driving the release roadmap; `docs/adr/` records architectural decisions; `docs/guides/` and `docs/agentic-aiops-platform/` provide operator/user/architecture documentation.
- The root `Makefile` is the orchestration layer: it enumerates `PYTHON_PRODUCTS` and `IMAGE_PRODUCTS`, delegates per-product `sync/test/lint/build/push` via `make -C products/$p`, renders Kustomize overlays (`overlays`), validates policies/dashboards/version lockstep (`validate-policy`, `validate-dashboards`, `validate-version`, `validate-secret-vocabulary`, `validate-password-policy`), and composes the pre-commit gate `verify`.
- Dependency direction is inward: products depend on `shared/shared-contracts` but never on each other directly; cross-service communication goes through the documented API/event contracts. `policy-center` is a design-stage stub — authorization is currently enforced in `tool-gateway` and `platform-gateway` until the policy center is implemented.
