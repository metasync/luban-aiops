Flat collection of Markdown guides under docs/guides/ that serve as the operator manual for the six-service platform (web-ui, platform-gateway, agent-service, tool-gateway, identity-service, Redis). README.md acts as the index linking to getting-started, configuration-reference, tool-configuration, architecture-overview, and troubleshooting. The guides are cross-referenced with sibling spec and delivery documents in ../agentic-aiops-platform and ../specs, and with the dev K8s overlay in shared/platform-ops/gitops, forming a top-level documentation entry point rather than an executable module.
