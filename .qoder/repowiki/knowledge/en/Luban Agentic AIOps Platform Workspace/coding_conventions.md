- Each product follows a uniform layout with `src/<service>/api/`, `core/`, `schemas/`, `services/`, optional `policies/` or `tools/`, plus `app.py`, `main.py`, and `metadata.py` entry points, mirrored by a parallel `tests/` directory.
- Every product ships its own `Makefile` exposing `sync`, `test`, `lint`, `build`, and `push` targets that delegate to `uv` and Docker, while the root Makefile only aggregates and coordinates cross-cutting concerns.
- Cross-product boundaries are expressed exclusively through JSON schemas and HTTP APIs defined in `shared/shared-contracts/schemas/` and validated at build time by scripts under `shared/shared-contracts/scripts/`.
- Feature work is governed by Spec-Driven Development: new capabilities require a `docs/specs/SPEC-NNN-<slug>/` triad (spec.md requirements, plan.md technical approach, tasks.md checklist) and ADRs for cross-boundary trust-model or architectural decisions.
- Policy configuration is centralized: a single canonical `policy-default.yaml` in `shared/shared-contracts/policies/` is copied into each consuming product via `make sync-policy`, and scenarios under `policies/` are validated through `validate_policy_scenarios`.
- Versioning is single-source-of-truth via the root `VERSION` file; `make validate-version` enforces lockstep between VERSION, per-product versions, and the portal, and coordinated image tags embed the semver prefix plus git SHA and dirty timestamp.
