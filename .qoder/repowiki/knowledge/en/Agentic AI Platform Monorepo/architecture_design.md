Each product under `products/` is an independent FastAPI service with its own Dockerfile, Makefile, and tests; cross-service communication is enforced through JSON Schema contracts in `shared/shared-contracts/schemas/` (agent chat, session, identity token, audit event, tool invocation) and policy documents in `shared/shared-contracts/policies/`. The `platform-gateway` acts as the single external entry point, delegating auth to `identity-broker`, routing agent requests to `agent-platform`, invoking tools via `tool-gateway`, and emitting every decision to `audit-service`. Runtime wiring is declarative: `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/` holds per-product Kustomize overlays plus runtime secret/config manifests, driven by scripts under `sync-*` and `deploy-overlay.sh`. Shared build infrastructure lives in `mk/` (defaults, image, python) and `shared/base-images/base-uv/`, while `docs/specs/SPEC-NNN/` and `docs/adr/` record the design decisions that bind the services together.
