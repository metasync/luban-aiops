The service is a FastAPI application bootstrapped by `main.py:run` (uvicorn entrypoint) which creates the app via `app.create_app()`. The `api/router.py` aggregates three route modules — `health`, `ingest`, `query` — mounted under a single FastAPI router. Business logic is split into `services/`: `audit_store.py` defines an `AuditStore` Protocol and two implementations (`InMemoryAuditStore` for dev/test, `PostgresAuditStore` over psycopg async connections), selected at runtime by `build_audit_store()` based on `AUDIT_STORE_BACKEND`; `retention.py` runs a background `asyncio.Task` loop that periodically evicts old events; `ingest_auth.py` validates incoming requests against static client credentials and projected workload tokens. Domain models live in `schemas/audit.py` as Pydantic v2 models (`AuditEvent`, `IngestRequest`, `AuditQuery`). Cross-cutting concerns are centralized in `core/`: `config.py` loads frozen `AuditSettings` from environment variables with `@lru_cache`, `metrics.py` exposes Prometheus gauges/counters, `telemetry.py` configures OpenTelemetry OTLP exporters, `observability.py` provides structured logging helpers, and `request_context.py` resolves per-request IDs. Lifecycle is managed through FastAPI's `lifespan` context manager, which initializes the store, starts the retention task, and tears them down on shutdown. Kubernetes deployment is declared in `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/base/audit-service/` as a Deployment + Service consuming ConfigMaps and Secrets via `envFrom`, exposing `/health/live` and `/health/ready` probes on port 8000.
