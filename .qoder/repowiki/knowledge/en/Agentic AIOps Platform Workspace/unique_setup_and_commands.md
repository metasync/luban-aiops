`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate running every product test suite, Kustomize overlay rendering, policy validation against the canonical `policy-default.yaml`, and version lockstep checks between `VERSION` and all products. `make build` produces images with a coordinated tag derived from `VERSION` + git SHA and writes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `make deploy` invokes the dev overlay's `deploy.sh`; `make e2e` runs demo scripts against a deployed cluster after port-forwarding `platform-gateway` and `identity-service`; `make sync-policy` copies the canonical policy bundle into every consumer location; `make base-images` builds the shared `luban-aiops/base-uv` image.
