Use the root Makefile as the single entry point: `make verify` runs every product's tests plus Kustomize overlay rendering, policy validation, and version lockstep checks; `make build` builds all images with a coordinated tag and writes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `make deploy` invokes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh`; `make e2e` runs demo scripts against a deployed cluster after port-forwarding `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081`; `make sync-policy` copies the canonical policy bundle to all consumers; per-product commands are available via `make -C products/<name> help`.
