`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate running every product's tests, Kustomize overlay rendering, policy validation, and version lockstep checks. `make build` tags all images uniformly and writes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `make deploy` invokes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh` against the current cluster. `make e2e` runs shell demos (`skills-demo.sh`, `incident-demo.sh`, `mutating-demo.sh`) after port-forwarding `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081`. Policy and version synchronization use `make sync-policy` and `make validate-version`.
