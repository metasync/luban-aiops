`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate running every product test suite plus Kustomize overlay rendering, policy validation, and version lockstep checks. `make build` builds all product images with a coordinated tag derived from `VERSION` and writes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `make deploy` invokes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh`; `make e2e` runs `incident-demo.sh`, `skills-demo.sh`, and `mutating-demo.sh` against a deployed cluster after port-forwarding `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081`. Per-product commands are available via `make -C products/<name> help`.
