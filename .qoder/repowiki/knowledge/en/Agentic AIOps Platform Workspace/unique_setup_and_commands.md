`make verify` is the pre-commit/pre-push gate running all product tests, kustomize overlay rendering, policy validation, and version lockstep checks. `make build` builds every product image with a coordinated tag derived from `VERSION` and writes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`. `make deploy` runs `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh` against the current cluster. `make e2e` executes demo scripts that require port-forwarding `platform-gateway` on 18083 and `identity-service` on 18081. Per-product routines are invoked via `make -C products/<name> help`.
