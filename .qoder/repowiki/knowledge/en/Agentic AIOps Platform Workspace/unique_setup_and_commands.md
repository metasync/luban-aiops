All workflows flow through the root `Makefile`: `make verify` runs every product's test suite, renders all Kustomize overlays, validates the canonical policy bundle, and enforces version lockstep; `make build` builds every product image with a coordinated semver tag derived from `VERSION` and writes `.images.env`; `make deploy` invokes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh`; `make e2e` runs demo scripts against a deployed cluster after port-forwarding `platform-gateway` and `identity-service`; `make sync-policy` copies the canonical policy to all consumer locations; `make validate-version` checks VERSION consistency across the repo.
