Use the root `Makefile` as the single entry point: `make verify` runs every product's test suite, renders all Kustomize overlays, validates the canonical policy bundle, and checks version lockstep; `make build` builds all images with a coordinated tag derived from `VERSION` and writes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `make deploy` invokes `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/deploy.sh`; `make e2e` runs `skills-demo.sh`, `incident-demo.sh`, and `mutating-demo.sh` against a deployed dev cluster after port-forwarding `platform-gateway:18083` and `identity-service:18081`. Per-product routines are available via `make -C products/<name> help`.
