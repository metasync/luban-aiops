`make verify` runs the full pre-commit gate (product tests, Kustomize overlay rendering, policy validation); `make build` builds all images with one coordinated tag and writes `IMAGE_TAG` plus per-image names to `shared/platform-ops/gitops/dev-k8s/.images.env`; `make deploy` applies the dev-k8s overlay; `make sync-policy` copies the canonical policy bundle into every consumer location.
